# Cinch's Missing Blocks

Cinch's Missing Blocks aims to add all the block variants that Mojang skipped. From random wall and stair variants that are missing to entire new brick sets. There are tons of new building blocks made from vanilla blocks. All these new blocks are fully functional with recipes, and they match the mechanics of the other blocks from the vanilla block set they belong to.

## Features

### 439 Total Blocks

**Calcite Blocks:** Polished, bricks, cracked bricks, and mossy bricks variants as well as chiseled calcite bricks and calcite brick pillars. Normal calcite now has slabs, stairs, and walls too.

**Dripstone Blocks:** Polished, bricks, cracked bricks, and mossy bricks variants as well as chiseled dripstone bricks and dripstone brick pillars. Normal dripstone now has slabs, stairs, and walls too.

**Tuff Blocks:** Mossy and cracked variants of tuff bricks. If you enable the enableTuffBrickPillar config, chiseled tuff bricks become the tuff brick pillar.

**Andesite Blocks:** Bricks, cracked bricks, and mossy brick variants as well as chiseled andesite bricks and andesite brick pillars. Also, the missing polished andesite wall.

**Granite Blocks:** Bricks, cracked bricks, and mossy brick variants as well as chiseled granite bricks and granite brick pillars. Also, the missing polished granite wall.

**Diorite Blocks:** Bricks, cracked bricks, and mossy brick variants as well as chiseled diorite bricks and diroite brick pillars. Also, the missing polished diorite wall.

**Quartz Blocks:** Quartz bricks now have slabs, stairs, and walls as well as cracked and mossy variants. The missing quartz wall and smooth quartz wall are also added.

**Brick Blocks:** Bricks now have cracked, mossy, and chiseled variants.

**Mud Blocks:** Packed mud now has slabs, stairs, and walls. Mud Bricks now have cracked and mossy variants, as well as chiseled mud bricks and mud brick pillars.

**Resin Blocks:** Mossy and cracked resin bricks.

**Sandstone Blocks:** Bricks, cracked bricks, and mossy brick variants as well as sandstone brick pillars. Cut sandstone now has stairs and walls and smooth red sandstone now has walls.

**Red Sandstone Blocks:** Bricks, cracked bricks, and mossy brick variants as well as red sandstone brick pillars. Cut red sandstone now has stairs and walls and smooth red sandstone now has walls.

**Soul Sandstone Blocks:** Soul sandstone, cut soul sandstone, and smooth soul sandsone all with stairs, slabs, and walls. Bricks and cracked bricks as well as soul sandstone brick pillars and chiseled soul sandstone. These blocks trigger the soul speed enchantment and produce blue fire.

**Cinnabar Blocks:** Cracked bricks, mossy bricks, and sulfur brick pillars.

**Sulfur Blocks:** Cracked bricks, mossy bricks, and cinnabar brick pillars.

**End Stone Blocks:** Polished end stone, cracked end stone bricks, and mossy end stone as well as chiseled endstone bricks and end stone brick pillars. End stone also now has slabs, stairs, and walls.

**Stone Blocks:** Stone tiles with cracked and mossy variants. Stone walls, smooth stone stairs and walls, and stone pillars.

**Deepslate Blocks:** Mossy cobbled deepslate, mossy bricks, mossy tiles, and deepslate brick pillars. Normal deepslate now has slabs, stairs, and walls. There are also polished deepslate buttons and pressure plates and a new sculk inlaid deepslate block.

**Blackstone Blocks:** Blackstone tiles and cracked tiles. There are also blackstone brick pillars.

**Netherrack Blocks:** Netherack now has slabs, stairs, and walls.

**Nether Bricks Blocks:** Nether brick fence gate and pillars.

**Red Nether Bricks Blocks:** Red nether bricks now have a cracked variant and a chiseled variant. The missing red nether brick fence is also added.

**Blue Nether Bricks Blocks:** Blue nether bricks with cracked, chiseled variants, and pillar variants.

**Nether Brick Items:** Red and blue nether brick items.

**Prismarine Blocks:** Cracked prismarine bricks, mossy prismarine bricks, chiseled prismarine bricks, prismarine brick pillars, and the missing prismarine brick wall and dark prismarine wall.

**Snow Blocks:** Snow bricks (Not present in 26.3+ since mojang added these blocks).

**Basalt Blocks:** Smooth basalt now has slabs, stairs, and walls.

**Purpur Blocks:** Purpur now has walls and a chiseled variant.

**Glass Blocks:** Tinted glass pane that blocks partial light.

**Concrete Blocks:** Slabs, stairs, and walls for concrete (Slabs and stairs not present in 26.2+ since mojang added these blocks).

**Terracotta Blocks:** Slabs, stairs, and walls for terracotta as well as a glazed version of regular terracotta.

**Cracked Variants:** All vanilla cracked brick blocks now have slabs, stairs, and walls. This applies to: cracked stone bricks, cracked deepslate bricks, cracked polished blackstone bricks, and cracked nether bricks. All three nether brick colors also have cracked variants of the fences and fence gates.

**Cinch's Double Slab Pack:** Built-in togglable texture pack that gives some slabs a custom double slab texture just like smooth stone slabs. Disabled by default.

**Fixed Sandstone Wall Textures:** Sandstone and red sandstone walls now have the correct top and bottom textures. This fix is applied to all appropriate modded sandstone walls and shouldn't break texture packs since only the models are changed, not the textures themselves.

**Recipes:** Added a new set of recipes for making mossy blocks that now let you use 2 moss carpets in place of the normal moss block or vine.

**Built-In Mod Compat:** Two of my other mods, Cinch's Villager Statues and Cinch's Lost Mob Grinders will sometimes spawn with modded blocks when using them alongside Cinch's Missing Blocks.

### NOTES:
- All polished, brick, cracked brick, mossy brick, and tile variants added come with slabs stairs and walls.
- All solid blocks are also sulfur cube compatible in 26.2+ and are part of the right sulfur cube archetypes.
- A few vanilla recipes are changed either to fit in new blocks or fix inconsistencies.

## Configs

All configs accept two possible values: (True/False)

**enableTerracottaVariants** - Can be used to enable/disable all terracotta stairs, slabs, and walls.

**enableConcreteVariants** - Can be used to enable/disable all concrete stairs, slabs, and walls. Only the walls are added in 26.3 and higher since Mojang added the stairs and slabs.

**enableTuffBrickPillar** - When set to true, Chiseled Tuff Bricks is renamed to Tuff Brick Pillar and is remade into a pillar block. Chiseled Tuff is renamed to Chiseled Tuff Bricks and the recipes for both blocks are changed.

**enableReworkedDeepslateRecipes** - When enabled, most deepslate recipes require normal deepslate rather than cobbled deepslate to better match stone recipes.

**enableCorrectedCobbledDrops** - When enabled, stone stairs, slabs, walls, and deepslate stairs, slabs, and walls drop their cobbled counterparts when mined without silk touch.

**doubleSlabsPackDefaultEnabled** - When set to true, the Cinch's Double Slabs pack is enabled by default. This setting is just for modpack makers.

Many more configs are planned.

## Compatibility

There is built in extra compatibility for the following mods:
- **Cinch's Villager Statues**
- **Cinch's Lost Mob Grinders**

There should be no compatibility issues for any of my other mods or most other mods unless they add some of the same blocks.

Extra built in compatibility is planned for some of my other mods.
